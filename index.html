癌症康复金与长者照护金，
只能二选一。Cancer recovery and elder care: it is one or the other.

所以，晚年的另一重守护，宜由 OAD 附加险来承接。That is why the OAD rider is the natural second layer for later life.

平保额，还是递增保额？Level cover or increasing cover?

保额 − 账户价值Rider Sum Assured − Account Value

平保额：扣除账户价值后赔付Level: payout after account value is deducted

理赔金额为附加险保额，减去理赔获批后下一估值日的账户价值。The payout is the rider sum assured less the account value at the next valuation date after claim approval.

账户价值越高，理赔金额可能越低。A higher account value may mean a lower payout.

常见问题Frequently asked questions

我有医药卡，还需要吗？I have a medical card. Do I need this?
医药卡主要付医疗和住院费，不一定补偿休养期间的收入中断、房贷或看护开支。Medical cards cover treatment costs, not necessarily lost income, mortgages or care costs.
癌症康复金和长者照护金会重复吗？Can I claim cancer recovery and elder care together?
不会。两者同属一项附赠保障，申领其中一项后，整项附赠保障终止。No. They form one complimentary benefit; a claim on either ends the entire benefit.
长者照护金和 OAD 会重复吗？Do elder care and OAD overlap?
两者不同：长者照护金是主险附赠、60 岁后按年给付；OAD 是附加险、65 岁后一次性赔付。实际理赔以条款为准。They differ: elder care is an included annual benefit after 60; OAD is an optional lump-sum rider after 65.
投资回报有保证吗？Are returns guaranteed?
没有。这是投资连结保单，账户价值随基金表现波动。No. This is an investment-linked policy; account value fluctuates with fund performance.
